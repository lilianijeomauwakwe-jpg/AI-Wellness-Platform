## Project Context

This project involved the Agile development and delivery of an AI-enabled wellness product designed to provide users with accessible digital wellness experiences and personalized support.

The product team had an established product vision but needed a structured delivery framework to translate that vision into organized, trackable development work.

I joined the project as Technical Project Manager & Scrum Master and established the Agile delivery structure used during the documented development phase.

---

## The Delivery Challenge

At the beginning of the engagement, the team faced several delivery challenges:

- No structured product backlog
- No established delivery framework
- Unclear team roles and ownership
- Limited visibility into delivery progress
- Risk of scope creep
- Cross-functional dependencies affecting delivery

The primary objective was to create a predictable delivery process that allowed the team to organize work, identify blockers early, and maintain visibility across the product development lifecycle.

## Delivery Structure

The project was delivered across two two-week Agile sprints.

### Sprint 1

**Sprint Goal:**  
Complete the user onboarding flow and core AI symptom-checker MVP.

The sprint was structured around a defined goal, prioritized backlog, story-point estimation, ownership, Agile ceremonies and delivery tracking.

### Sprint 2

**Sprint Goal:**  
Complete the provider directory, appointment booking flow and notification system.

For the second sprint, I strengthened ticket quality by ensuring detailed acceptance criteria were included across the sprint backlog.

## Key Delivery Challenges Resolved

### Third-Party API Dependency

A delay involving a third-party API created a potential delivery dependency.

I coordinated the issue, tracked the dependency and worked with the relevant team members to resolve the blocker without disrupting the documented sprint delivery timeline.

### Design Dependency Conflict

A conflict involving design dependencies also had the potential to affect delivery.

The dependency was identified and coordinated during the sprint, allowing the team to continue progressing against the sprint goal.
