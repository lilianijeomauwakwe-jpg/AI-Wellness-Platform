# Product Requirements

## AI Wellness Platform

### Requirements Structure & Product Scope

This document demonstrates how product requirements were organized and translated into actionable Agile development work during the documented product delivery phase.

The product backlog was structured around key product capabilities and broken down into epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, and estimated delivery effort.

---

## Product Scope

The documented product backlog was organized around five primary product areas:

| Epic | Product Area |
|---|---|
| 01 | User Onboarding |
| 02 | AI Diagnostics |
| 03 | Provider Directory |
| 04 | Appointment Booking |
| 05 | Notifications |

The initial product backlog contained **24 user stories**.

---

## Requirements Management Approach

The requirements process included:

1. Breaking product capabilities into epics
2. Translating product needs into user stories
3. Defining acceptance criteria
4. Estimating effort using story points
5. Prioritizing work for sprint delivery
6. Establishing a Definition of Done
7. Reviewing requirements during sprint planning
8. Refining requirements as delivery progressed

---

## User Story Structure

User stories followed the standard Agile format:

> **As a [user], I want [capability], so that [benefit].**

This helped connect individual development work to the intended user outcome.

### Example

```text
As a user,
I want to complete my onboarding information,
so that I can access a personalized experience.
