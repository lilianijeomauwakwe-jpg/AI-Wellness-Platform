# UX & Product Collaboration

## AI Wellness Platform

### Product Thinking, UX Collaboration & Cross-Functional Delivery

This document demonstrates how I worked across Product, UX/UI, Engineering and QA to translate product requirements into a deliverable product experience.

---

## My Role in Product Collaboration

As Technical Project Manager & Scrum Master, I worked at the intersection of product requirements and technical delivery.

My responsibilities included:

- Translating requirements into actionable delivery work
- Coordinating Product and Engineering
- Supporting UX/UI alignment
- Tracking design dependencies
- Clarifying requirements
- Supporting acceptance readiness
- Maintaining delivery visibility

---

## UX/UI Collaboration

Figma was used as the primary UX/UI reference during delivery.

My role was not to replace the UX/UI designer, but to ensure that design work and delivery requirements remained aligned.

This included:

- Tracking design dependencies
- Connecting design work to Jira delivery items
- Identifying design-related blockers
- Supporting communication between Design and Engineering
- Ensuring requirements were sufficiently clear for implementation
