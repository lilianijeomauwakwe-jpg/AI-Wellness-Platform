# Risk & Dependency Management

## AI Wellness Platform

### Identifying, Tracking & Resolving Delivery Risks

This document demonstrates how delivery risks and cross-functional dependencies were identified and managed during the documented Agile development phase.

---

## Risk Management Approach

My approach focused on identifying risks early, understanding their potential impact on sprint commitments, assigning appropriate ownership, and tracking issues through resolution.

The main risk categories included:

- Technical dependencies
- Design dependencies
- Scope changes
- Requirements ambiguity
- Delivery blockers

---

## Technical Dependency: Third-Party API

A third-party API integration delay emerged during the documented delivery period.

### Potential Impact

The dependency had the potential to delay work that relied on the integration and affect sprint progress.

### Action Taken

I coordinated the dependency with the relevant team members, tracked the blocker and maintained visibility until the issue was resolved.

### Outcome

The blocker was resolved without disrupting the documented sprint delivery timeline.

## Design Dependency

A design dependency conflict also emerged during delivery.

### Potential Impact

The conflict had the potential to delay implementation work and create downstream delivery impact.

### Action Taken

I identified the dependency, coordinated the relevant stakeholders and maintained visibility until the issue was addressed.

### Outcome

The team continued progressing against the sprint objective without disruption to the documented delivery timeline.

## Scope Risk

New requests can create delivery risk when they are introduced during an active sprint.

### Risk

Adding unplanned work directly into a committed sprint can:

- Reduce focus
- Affect sprint capacity
- Delay committed work
- Create unpredictable delivery

### Approach

New requests were captured in the product backlog rather than automatically added to the active sprint.

This protected the sprint commitment while ensuring that new requirements were not lost.

### Outcome

The documented project recorded **0 scope-creep incidents** across the two sprints.

---

## Requirements Risk

Unclear requirements can result in:

- Development rework
- Misunderstanding between Product and Engineering
- QA ambiguity
- Delayed acceptance

### Mitigation

I introduced stronger requirements structure through:

- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Definition of Done
- Sprint goals
- Backlog organization

For Sprint 2, detailed acceptance criteria were added across the sprint backlog.
