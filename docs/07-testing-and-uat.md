# Testing & UAT

## AI Wellness Platform

### Quality, Acceptance & Delivery Readiness

This document demonstrates how quality and acceptance considerations were incorporated into the Agile delivery process.

---

## My Role

As Technical Project Manager & Scrum Master, I coordinated requirements, acceptance expectations, delivery readiness and cross-functional communication between Product, Engineering, Design and QA.

My responsibilities included:

- Clarifying acceptance expectations
- Ensuring acceptance criteria were documented
- Supporting QA readiness
- Coordinating Product and QA feedback
- Tracking issues and blockers
- Supporting sprint acceptance

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria were used to define the expected outcome of user stories.

They provided a shared reference for:

- Engineering
- QA
- Product
- Project delivery

For Sprint 2, I ensured detailed acceptance criteria were included across the sprint backlog.

This helped reduce ambiguity and provided clearer validation expectations.

---

## Definition of Done

A Definition of Done was established as a shared standard for completed work.

It helped ensure that work was not treated as complete simply because development activity had finished.

The Definition of Done supported alignment across:

- Development
- Design
- QA
- Product
- Project delivery

---

## QA Collaboration

QA was part of the cross-functional delivery team.

I supported QA collaboration by ensuring that:

- Requirements were sufficiently clear
- Acceptance criteria were available
- Delivery expectations were understood
- Product feedback could be incorporated
- Blockers were surfaced quickly

---
