# Stakeholder Management

## AI Wellness Platform

### Communication, Alignment & Delivery Visibility

This document demonstrates how stakeholder communication and cross-functional alignment were managed during the documented Agile delivery phase.

---

## Stakeholder Groups

The project involved coordination across several stakeholder and delivery groups:

| Stakeholder | Primary Concern |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Product priorities, requirements and acceptance |
| Engineering | Technical implementation and dependencies |
| UX/UI | Product experience and design dependencies |
| QA | Quality and acceptance readiness |
| Project Delivery | Timeline, scope, risks and coordination |

---

## My Role

As Technical Project Manager & Scrum Master, I acted as a coordination point between Product and the cross-functional delivery team.

My responsibilities included:

- Maintaining delivery visibility
- Coordinating stakeholder communication
- Providing Product Owner updates
- Surfacing risks and blockers
- Facilitating Agile ceremonies
- Capturing feedback
- Coordinating cross-functional dependencies
- Tracking follow-up actions
